MARKET CONTEXT · India VIX vs model's realized-vol forecast

India VIX is the market's own options-implied volatility gauge. The model's HAR-RV forecast estimates realized volatility from price history. The gap between them is the variance risk premium -- the thing the sell-ATM baseline was actually harvesting, not a mispricing the model found.

PUT-CALL RATIO · open-interest based

Total put OI / total call OI for the nearest expiry, computed from the same bhavcopy file already used for pricing. A commonly-cited positioning gauge -- elevated PCR is conventionally read as more hedging/bearish positioning -- but this is context, not a signal this pipeline has validated.

What this panel is, and isn't: VIX, PCR, and Max Pain are real, NSE-published figures, not synthetic -- but they are market-conditions context, not trading signals this pipeline has backtested or validated. Nothing on this page has demonstrated a real, tradeable edge (see the backtest and paper-trading panels above). Deliberately excluded: FII/DII flows, global cues (Dow, crude, SGX/GIFT Nifty), news sentiment, and anything positioned as a "market projection" -- these either require data sources beyond this free NSE pipeline, or risk exactly the false-confidence trap this project spent many rounds debugging out of. More charts do not create more edge.
All figures on this page come from synthetic data generated by probability_model.py, backtest.py, and adaptive_model.py — this validates the pipeline's logic and wiring, not a real-market edge. Swap in fetch_nse_historical.py output before drawing conclusions about actual Nifty markets.
